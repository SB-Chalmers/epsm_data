Below is the office recipe structure for all three scenarios. The baseline layers are retained in renovated and advanced scenarios unless an intervention below says otherwise.

The current office archetypes use two baseline families:

- P01–P02: masonry office with an unheated basement and cold attic.
- P03–P09: concrete office with slab foundation, concrete intermediate floors, and a warm roof.

The window values below should be replaced by the newer office matrix assumptions you provided:

| EPSM period | Office baseline `Uw` |
|---|---:|
| P01–P02 | 2.24 |
| P03 | 2.24 |
| P04 | 2.06 |
| P05 | 2.06 or 1.82 depending on year mapping |
| P06 | 1.82 or 1.99 depending on year mapping |
| P07–P09 | 1.99 or 1.95 |

These are assumed whole-window values, not source-verified universal values.

## P01 — before 1945

Baseline external wall:

- 15 mm interior plaster
- 510 mm solid brick
- 20 mm exterior render

Baseline ground/basement floor:

- 100 mm concrete basement slab
- 3 mm bituminous damp-proofing, thermally omitted
- 150 mm gravel subbase, omitted

Baseline buried basement wall:

- 15 mm internal plaster
- 380 mm brick
- 3 mm external damp-proofing, omitted

Baseline exposed basement wall:

- 15 mm internal plaster
- 380 mm brick
- 15 mm plinth render

Baseline basement ceiling:

- 25 mm timber floorboards
- 220 mm joist zone:
  - 15% timber
  - 85% wood shavings
- 22 mm ceiling boards
- 15 mm ceiling plaster

Baseline intermediate floor:

- 28 mm timber floorboards
- 220 mm joist zone:
  - 15% timber
  - 85% wood shavings
- 22 mm ceiling boards
- 15 mm plaster lining

Baseline internal partition:

- 15 mm plaster
- 8 mm timber lath
- 70 mm timber/wood-shaving zone
- 8 mm timber lath
- 15 mm plaster

Baseline attic floor:

- 15 mm plaster
- 22 mm timber ceiling boards
- 0.2 mm paper air/vapour layer, omitted
- 150 mm joist zone:
  - 15% timber
  - 85% wood shavings

Baseline roof shell:

- 195 mm rafter/air zone, omitted
- 22 mm roof boarding, omitted
- 5 mm bituminous waterproofing, omitted

Renovated:

- retain brick and structural basement;
- add 100 mm external mineral-wool facade insulation to the wall;
- add new reinforced render;
- add 100 mm attic mineral wool above the attic-floor assembly;
- retain basement and intermediate-floor structures;
- replace windows with `Uw = 1.2`;
- retain roof shell.

Advanced:

- replace the 100 mm wall insulation addition with 180 mm facade mineral wool;
- add new wind/rainscreen or reinforced-render detailing as appropriate;
- add 200 mm attic mineral wool;
- add 80 mm external basement-wall EPS where applicable;
- add 140 mm basement-ceiling mineral wool plus 13 mm gypsum fire lining;
- replace windows with `Uw = 0.9`.

- facade mineral wool: proxy `6000000125`;
- roof mineral wool: proxy `6000000127`;
- mineral wool: proxy `6000000123`;
- EPS: exact `6000000131`;
- gypsum: exact `6000000020`;
- polyethylene membrane: exact `6000000198`;
- render: proxy `6000000093`;
- bituminous membrane: proxy `6000000141`;
- replacement windows: unresolved because windows are represented by whole-element `Uw`, not a selected Boverket window product.

All retained baseline materials remain visible in the crosswalk, but they are outside the Boverket intervention requirement. Honeybee identifiers are existing library templates; they do not establish product equivalence or historical identity.
