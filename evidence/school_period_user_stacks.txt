Below is the current **school baseline, renovated and advanced structure**, with the material crosswalk. The baseline stacks are the same school families described earlier; renovated and advanced scenarios derive from those baselines through explicit interventions.

The source-matrix window assumptions should be used as the school baseline central values:

| EPSM period | School baseline Uw |
|---|---:|
| P01–P02, ≤1960 | 2.80 |
| P03, 1961–1970 | 2.70 |
| P04, 1971–1980 | 2.70 or 2.25 depending on mapping |
| P05, 1981–1990 | 2.25 |
| P06, 1991–2000 | 1.90 or 1.60 |
| P07–P09, 2001 onward | 1.14 |

These are assumed whole-window values from the source matrix, not universal measured values.

## P01 — before 1945

Baseline family: solid masonry school with unheated basement and cold attic.

External wall:

- 15 mm interior plaster
- 380 mm brick masonry
- 20 mm exterior render

Attic floor:

- 15 mm plaster
- 22 mm timber ceiling boards
- 0.2 mm paper air/vapour layer, omitted
- 150 mm joist zone:
  - 15% timber
  - 85% wood shavings

Ground/basement slab:

- 100 mm concrete
- 3 mm bituminous damp-proofing, omitted
- 150 mm gravel subbase, omitted

Basement walls:

- 15 mm internal plaster
- 380 mm brick
- 3 mm external damp-proofing on buried areas, omitted
- 15 mm exposed plinth render

Basement ceiling:

- 25 mm timber floorboards
- 220 mm joist zone:
  - 15% timber
  - 85% wood shavings
- 22 mm ceiling boards
- 15 mm plaster

Intermediate floor:

- 28 mm timber floorboards
- 220 mm joist zone:
  - 15% timber
  - 85% wood shavings
- 22 mm ceiling boards
- 15 mm plaster lining

Internal partition:

- 15 mm plaster
- 8 mm timber lath
- 70 mm timber/wood-shaving zone
- 8 mm timber lath
- 15 mm plaster

Roof shell:

- 195 mm rafter/air zone, omitted
- 22 mm roof boarding, omitted
- 5 mm bituminous waterproofing, omitted

Renovated:

- add 100 mm external mineral-wool facade insulation;
- add reinforced exterior render;
- add 100 mm attic mineral wool;
- add 80 mm external basement-wall EPS where applicable;
- add 80 mm basement-ceiling mineral wool;
- add 13 mm new gypsum fire lining below the basement ceiling;
- replace windows with `Uw = 1.2`.

Advanced:

- add 180 mm external facade mineral wool;
- add 200 mm attic insulation;
- add 120 mm basement-wall EPS;
- add 140 mm basement-ceiling mineral wool;
- retain structural masonry and timber floors;
- replace windows with `Uw = 0.9`.

For renovated and advanced school scenarios, incoming Boverket mappings are required for:

- facade mineral wool: proxy `6000000125`;
- mineral wool: proxy `6000000123`;
- EPS: exact `6000000131`;
- polyethylene vapour-control film: exact `6000000198`;
- wind barrier: exact `6000000199`;
- gypsum lining: exact `6000000020`;
- timber cladding: proxy `6000000007`;
- render: proxy `6000000093`;
- replacement windows: unresolved because the model stores whole-element `Uw`, not a selected Boverket window product.

The main evidence limitation remains that P03 is informed by actual Gävle school evidence, while most other school periods use explicit representative assumptions. Sörbyskolan and Fridhemsskolan provide useful material and retrofit context, but their audited or modernised states must not be labelled as universally original construction.
