Below are the current **MFH baseline** stacks in EPSM. They are the selected baseline constructions, before renovation. Thicknesses are in metres. Layers are listed from the conditioned side toward the exterior or adjacent space.

These are modelled representative stacks; only MFH-P03 has a source-specific construction replacement currently prepared in the review work.
