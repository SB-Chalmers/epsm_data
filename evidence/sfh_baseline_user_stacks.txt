Below are the current **SFH baseline stacks** in EPSM, followed by the material crosswalk. These are representative modelled constructions, not a claim that every Swedish single-family house used the same assembly.

Layer order is from the conditioned side toward the exterior or adjacent space. Framing and insulation in the same layer group share one depth.

## SFH-P01 — before 1945

External wall:

- 15 mm interior plaster
- 100 mm solid timber plank
- 0.3 mm historic paper air-control layer, omitted
- 0.3 mm paper wind barrier, omitted
- 25 mm ventilated batten cavity, omitted:
  - 10% timber
  - 90% air
- 22 mm timber cladding, omitted

Suspended floor:

- 28 mm timber floorboards
- 200 mm joist zone:
  - 15% timber
  - 85% wood shavings
- 22 mm subfloor boards
- 0.3 mm paper wind protection, omitted

Attic floor:

- 15 mm plaster ceiling
- 22 mm timber ceiling boards
- 0.2 mm paper air/vapour layer, omitted
- 150 mm ceiling-joist zone:
  - 15% timber
  - 85% wood shavings

Intermediate floor:

- 28 mm timber floorboards
- 220 mm joist zone:
  - 15% timber
  - 85% wood shavings
- 22 mm ceiling boards
- 15 mm plaster lining

Internal partition:

- 15 mm plaster
- 8 mm timber lath
- 70 mm partition zone:
  - 15% timber
  - 85% wood shavings
- 8 mm timber lath
- 15 mm plaster

Roof shell:

- 195 mm rafter/air zone, omitted
- 22 mm roof boarding, omitted
- 5 mm bituminous waterproofing, omitted

Window: `Uw = 2.8 W/(m²K)`.

## SFH-P04 to SFH-P09

These periods use the same timber-frame family. The period changes are the frame/infill depth, attic insulation depth, slab insulation depth and window performance.

| Period | Wall frame/infill | Attic insulation | Under-slab EPS | Mineral wool | Window Uw |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| P04, 1971–1980 | 145 mm | 200 mm | 70 mm | λ≈0.040 | 2.5 |
| P05, 1981–1990 | 170 mm | 250 mm | 100 mm | λ≈0.040 | 2.0 |
| P06, 1991–2000 | 195 mm | 300 mm | 150 mm | λ≈0.040 | 1.8 |
| P07, 2001–2010 | 220 mm | 350 mm | 200 mm | λ≈0.036 | 1.4 |
| P08, 2011–2020 | 245 mm | 400 mm | 250 mm | λ≈0.036 | 1.1 |
| P09, 2021–2026 | 245 mm | 400 mm | 250 mm | λ≈0.036 | 1.0 |

For each of P04–P09, the external wall is:

- 13 mm gypsum lining
- 0.2 mm polyethylene vapour-control layer, omitted
- period-specific parallel timber/mineral-wool frame above
- 0.2 mm wind barrier, omitted
- 25 mm ventilated cavity, omitted:
  - 10% timber
  - 90% air
- 22 mm timber cladding, omitted

The ground floor is:

- 10 mm timber finish
- 120 mm concrete slab
- period-specific EPS thickness
- 0.2 mm polyethylene damp-proofing, omitted
- 150 mm gravel subbase, omitted
- 0.2 mm geotextile, omitted

The attic floor is:

- 13 mm gypsum lining
- 22 mm timber ceiling boards
- 0.2 mm polyethylene vapour-control layer, omitted
- period-specific parallel timber/mineral-wool joist zone:
  - 15% timber
  - 85% mineral wool

Intermediate floor:

- 28 mm timber floorboards
- 220 mm joist zone:
  - 15% timber
  - 85% mineral wool
- 22 mm ceiling boards
- 13 mm gypsum lining

Internal partition:

- 13 mm gypsum
- 70 mm parallel timber/mineral-wool zone:
  - 15% timber
  - 85% mineral wool
- 13 mm gypsum

Roof shell for all periods:

- 195 mm rafter/air zone, omitted
- 22 mm roof boarding, omitted
- 5 mm bituminous waterproofing, omitted

## SFH material crosswalk

| EPSM material | Honeybee identifier | HB status | Boverket ID | Boverket status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Timber | `G05 25mm wood` | proxy | `6000000007` | proxy |
| Historic/mineral wool | `Wall Insulation [31]` | proxy | `6000000123` | proxy |
| EPS | `I02 50mm insulation board` | proxy | `6000000131` | exact |
| Gypsum | `G01 13mm gypsum board` | proxy | `6000000020` | exact |
| Interior plaster | `1IN Stucco` | proxy | `6000000094` | proxy |
| Bitumen | `Roof Membrane` | proxy | `6000000141` | proxy |
| Polyethylene film | unresolved | unresolved | `6000000198` | exact |
| Wind barrier | unresolved | unresolved | `6000000199` | exact |
| Wood shavings | unresolved | unresolved | `6000000135` | proxy |
| Paper air-control layer | unresolved | unresolved | unresolved | unresolved |
| Gravel | unresolved | unresolved | unresolved | unresolved |
| Geotextile | unresolved | unresolved | `6000000210` | proxy |
| Air cavity | `F04 Wall air space resistance` | proxy | not applicable | not applicable |
| Concrete | `M15 200mm heavyweight concrete` | proxy | `6000000026` | proxy |

Boverket is intentionally required only for incoming renovation materials. For SFH renovation and advanced scenarios, the current incoming mappings are:

- wind barrier: exact Boverket resource;
- EPS: exact Boverket resource;
- polyethylene film: exact Boverket resource;
- mineral wool facade board: Boverket proxy `6000000125`;
- mineral wool: proxy `6000000123`;
- timber cladding/wood: proxy `6000000007`;
- replacement windows: unresolved because the model stores whole-window `Uw`, not a selected Boverket window product.

The Honeybee entries are existing library templates. Their nominal thicknesses are not the installed SFH layer thicknesses, and the proxy status does not establish historical product equivalence.
